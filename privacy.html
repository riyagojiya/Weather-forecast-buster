PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This prototype is intended for demonstration and evaluation.

Information collected

The static prototype does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through these pages.

Third-party resources

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The India map image is loaded from Wikimedia Commons. The forecast viewer loads Leaflet from unpkg, PapaParse from jsDelivr, base map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and state boundaries from GitHub, so those services may receive your IP address and browser details.

Future integrations

If analytics, accounts, APIs, or other data services are added, this policy should be updated before deployment.
