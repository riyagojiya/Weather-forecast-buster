THE PROBLEM

Rapidly evolving weather systems can challenge medium-range forecasts.

Monsoon depressions, heavy rainfall, western disturbances, cyclones, heat waves, and active or break monsoon phases can produce forecast behaviour that differs substantially from historical patterns.

The system is designed to compare forecast patterns with historical forecast-error behaviour and surface where confidence should be treated with greater caution.
