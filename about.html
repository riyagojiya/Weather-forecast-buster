ABOUT AERIS

AI-based forecast bust detection for medium-range weather forecasts.

This prototype addresses Smart India Hackathon 2026 Problem Statement 26079, focused on identifying regions and lead times where medium-range forecasts may have higher uncertainty or larger error.

Problem statement26079
OrganizationMinistry of Earth Sciences
DepartmentNCMRWF
ThemeSmart Automation

CORE IDEA

Find where the forecast deserves more scrutiny.

The intended system learns from historical forecast-error behaviour and uses current forecast patterns to estimate confidence across regions and lead times. The dashboard then presents that output with an explanation layer.
